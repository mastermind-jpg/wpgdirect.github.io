Shipping & Logistics

Already buying overseas? We ship it for you.

Ship your purchases to our partner warehouse in China. We consolidate, clear customs, and deliver to your door in Winnipeg. Charged by actual weight only — not volumetric.

Most services charge by volume — a bag of clothes costs the same as bricks. We charge by actual weight, so light and bulky items cost a fraction of what you'd pay elsewhere.
